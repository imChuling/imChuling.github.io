HEARTBREAKER

Led Zeppelin Cover Lead Vocals Live Performance

A full-band cover of Led Zeppelin's Heartbreaker — lead vocals by Chuling Li, with Richard Bird on guitar.

About

Heartbreaker is one of the hardest Led Zeppelin tracks to pull off live — the vocal line sits right at the top of the range and demands raw energy throughout. This performance with Richard Bird captures that intensity: no safety net, just a band locked in and pushing.

As You Read

Max/RNBO Web Audio Interaction Design Generative Score

A generative sound layer that lives inside this portfolio. The site itself is the instrument — every scroll, hover, click, and pause shapes a real-time soundscape processed through a Max/RNBO engine running in the browser.

The idea

Most portfolio sites are silent. This one listens back. Rather than embedding a separate audio player, the entire browsing experience is scored in real time: where you go, how you move, how long you stay, and whether you come back all leave traces in the sound. The visitor is not an audience — they are a performer who may not realise they are playing.

Five sections, five soundscapes

Each section of the portfolio has its own harmonic world — a looping pad, a set of processing parameters, and a melodic scale. Crossing into a new section triggers a crossfade between sound beds, and the granular engine, overdrive, and reverb all shift to match.

  • Hero — D dorian, warm and open, moderate granular processing
  • Projects — A major, bright and light, short dense grains with lifted highs
  • Music — E dorian, the thickest and widest, heavy granular and overdrive
  • Story — Bb lydian, high and distant, long grains with deep reverb
  • About — Cmaj9, warm and close, processing pulled back

Browsing as performance

Over thirty interactions are mapped to sound. Scrolling steps through scale tones. Mouse position shifts pitch and stereo image. Hovering a card triggers a triplet figure. Clicking anything opens a four-note chord with a granular stutter. Selecting text makes the selection hum — a triangle wave at the root, brightening with every twelve characters. Wiping the frosted glass in the Story section adds a bandpass noise layer that follows your hand. Even stillness has a voice: after ten seconds of no movement, the melody begins arpeggating on its own.

Returning to a section you have already visited thickens the sound — more granular residue, longer reverb tails. The place remembers that you were there.

Signal chain

All audio — five looping sound beds, an FM bell melody generated in real time, and interaction-driven noise — is routed through a single processing chain exported from Max 9 via RNBO: a granular engine (JR-Granular), into overdrive, into plate reverb. The chain runs entirely in the browser. Every sound passes through the same room, so the result feels cohesive rather than layered.

The melody uses FM synthesis with four timbral ratios that rotate between bell, glass, hollow, and metallic characters. Notes follow a random walk along each section's seven-tone scale, with occasional octave leaps and response figures.

Tools

  • Max 9 / RNBO 1.3.5 — granular processing chain, exported to WebAssembly
  • Web Audio API — sound beds, FM melody synthesis, interaction sonification
  • JavaScript — all interaction-to-sound mapping and score logic

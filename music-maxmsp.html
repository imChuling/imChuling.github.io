About

This is an ongoing series of sound studies built in Max/MSP, exploring granular synthesis, spectral processing, and real-time audio manipulation. Each patch is a small experiment — a way to understand how sound behaves when you break it apart and reassemble it.

The work sits at the intersection of music technology and creative practice: building tools that respond to gesture and intention, not just parameters.

Techniques

  • Granular synthesis — fragmenting audio into micro-grains and recombining with variable density, pitch, and scatter
  • Spectral processing — FFT-based effects including spectral freeze, bin shifting, and cross-synthesis
  • Real-time control — mapping MIDI controllers and gesture to synthesis parameters
  • Signal flow design — building modular patches that can be chained and reconfigured live

Tools

  • Max/MSP — primary patching environment
  • RNBO — for web-exportable audio engines
  • Gen~ — for sample-level DSP

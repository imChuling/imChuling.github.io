About

I co-founded this band with Richard Bird in September 2023, and we have been performing together since. We started rooted in hard rock and blues, then gradually expanded into funk, R&B, and beyond — following wherever the music pulled us.

I sing lead vocals and play keyboards, with occasional live programming — triggering backing tracks or layered parts from stage when a song calls for it. The set list keeps shifting, but the energy stays the same: locked in with the band, reading the room, making decisions in real time.

Role

  • Lead vocals — main vocalist across the set
  • Keyboards — stage piano and synth patches
  • Live programming — triggering backing tracks and layered parts on stage
